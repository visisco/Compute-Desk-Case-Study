Methodology and governance

A benchmark you can settle a contract on

CDX futures settle on Compute Signal, so the index is held to the standard of a regulated benchmark.

Built from physical deals

The index uses physical deal prints and contributed quotes only. CDX futures prices never feed it, so trading the futures can't move their own settlement.

Published methodology

Every rule is public and versioned. Changes go through a 30-day consultation and are announced on the benchmark feed before they apply.

Independent oversight

An oversight committee with outside members, separate from the exchange's commercial team, aligned with IOSCO benchmark principles.

Input controls

Outlier filters and a minimum number of prints per fix. When prints are thin, a documented fallback applies and the value is flagged.

Corrections in the open

Values can be corrected until 12:00 New York the next business day. Corrections are broadcast, and history keeps both versions.

Confidential by design

Physical aggregates publish only cells with 3 or more counterparties, where none is more than half. Single deals are never shown.

Licensing

Plain answers on how you can use the data

One master data agreement covers every plan, with a short schedule for each use.

What's the difference between display and non-display?

Display means a person sees the data on a screen, and is counted per user. Non-display means a machine uses it: an algo, a risk or margin model, a valuation. That's licensed per firm.

When do I need a benchmark licence?

When you settle, price or value a financial product or loan against a Compute Signal index. That applies even if you get the numbers from a free plan.

Can I build my own models and indicators?

Yes, for internal use. A derived product that could replace the index or the feed, such as another published index or curve, needs a separate licence.

Can I show the data to my clients?

Only through a redistribution licence, or as delayed data with attribution. Public uses cite "Source: Compute Signal" or "Source: Compute Desk Exchange" with the as-of time.

Is delayed data free to use?

Yes, for any use except benchmark use and redistribution.

How is usage checked?

Redistributors report users monthly. We can audit usage once a year, and under-reported usage is billed at list price.

What happens when an index value is corrected?

You get a correction message on the stream and the benchmark feed. History keeps the first published value and the correction, so a backtest sees what traders saw on the day.

Does physical deal data reveal who traded?

No. Only aggregates are published, and only where at least 3 counterparties contribute and none is more than half of the cell.
